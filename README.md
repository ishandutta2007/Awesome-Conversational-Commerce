# Awesome-Conversational-Commerce

# Top Conversational Commerce Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on AI Chat, Live Chat, Ecommerce Messaging, Shopping Assistants, Helpdesk & Revenue-Oriented Conversations*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Conversational Commerce**. These systems power chat and messaging experiences that help brands sell, support, and convert—across web, social, WhatsApp, and in-app channels—often with AI agents that can take actions like order lookup or cart updates.

**Examples** include Gorgias, Heyday by Hootsuite, Rep AI, Tidio, Intercom Fin, Drift, LivePerson, ManyChat, Salesloft Conversations, and Quiq (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Conversational commerce has strong open alternatives. **Chatwoot**, **Rasa**, and related projects enable self-hosted live chat, omnichannel desks, and custom shopping bots. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Gorgias](https://www.gorgias.com/)**  
  Leading ecommerce helpdesk and AI chat platform tightly integrated with Shopify for support and order-related conversational commerce.

- **[Heyday by Hootsuite](https://www.hootsuite.com/)**  
  Conversational commerce and messaging capabilities within the Hootsuite ecosystem for social and chat-driven shopping.

- **[Rep AI](https://www.rep.ai/)**  
  AI agent and live chat platform oriented toward ecommerce conversion and customer conversations.

- **[Tidio](https://www.tidio.com/)**  
  AI chatbot and live chat platform popular with SMBs and Shopify stores for sales and support conversations.

- **[Intercom Fin](https://www.intercom.com/)**  
  Intercom’s AI agent (Fin) and messaging suite for multi-channel support and conversational experiences at scale.

- **[Drift / Salesloft Conversations](https://www.salesloft.com/)**  
  Conversational marketing and sales chat platform (Drift lineage) focused on revenue and meeting conversion.

- **[LivePerson](https://www.liveperson.com/)**  
  Enterprise conversational AI and messaging platform for large-scale customer engagement and commerce use cases.

- **[ManyChat](https://manychat.com/)**  
  Chat marketing platform widely used for Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp automated conversational campaigns.

- **[Quiq](https://quiq.com/)**  
  Conversational AI platform for messaging-based customer engagement across modern channels.

- **[Related ecommerce chat apps](https://www.gorgias.com/)**  
  Broader category includes many Shopify-centric chat and AI shopping assistant apps.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot)**  
  Leading open-source omnichannel customer support and live-chat platform—strong alternative to Intercom/Zendesk with Shopify integrations and AI agent options.

- **[Rasa](https://github.com/RasaHQ/rasa)**  
  Open-source conversational AI framework for building contextual text and voice assistants, including ecommerce support bots.

- **[Botpress](https://github.com/botpress/botpress)**  
  Open-source chatbot and conversational AI platform for building, deploying, and managing multi-channel bots.

- **[Typebot / open form-chat builders](https://github.com/)**  
  Open tools for conversational forms and chat-style flows useful for lead capture and simple commerce journeys.

- **[Rocket.Chat](https://github.com/RocketChat/Rocket.Chat)**  
  Open-source team and customer messaging platform that can be extended for support and conversational use cases.

- **[Mattermost / open messaging cores](https://github.com/mattermost)**  
  Open collaboration platforms sometimes adapted for customer-facing conversational channels.

- **[Ecommerce chatbot example projects](https://github.com/)**  
  Community Rasa and Botpress projects for order tracking, FAQs, returns, and product lookup.

- **[WhatsApp / messaging channel open bridges](https://github.com/)**  
  Open connectors and APIs for connecting self-hosted bots to WhatsApp, Telegram, and similar channels.

- **[Documentation and Chatwoot / Rasa playbooks](https://www.chatwoot.com/docs/)**  
  Guides for deploying self-hosted conversational desks and training commerce-oriented assistants.

- **[Self-hosted stack patterns](https://github.com/)**  
  Chatwoot + Rasa/Botpress + ecommerce API integrations for full ownership of conversational commerce data.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Deploying **Chatwoot** as the primary helpdesk and live-chat layer for ecommerce brands.
- Building custom shopping and support assistants with **Rasa** or **Botpress**.
- Connecting open bots to Shopify/order APIs for real transactional actions.
- Accepting that polished AI resolution billing, deep Shopify-native actions at scale, enterprise governance, and multi-brand networks still favor commercial platforms (Gorgias, Intercom Fin, Tidio, Drift/Salesloft, LivePerson, ManyChat, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, cost control, and custom dialogue logic.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Run Chatwoot for human + AI inbox → add Rasa/Botpress for complex flows → integrate order and catalog APIs → measure conversion and resolution. Suitable for brands that want full control. Many high-growth ecommerce teams still choose commercial conversational commerce platforms for speed and integrations.
